## QUALITY STANDARDS

### Accuracy Requirements
- **Source Verification**: Minimum 2 independent sources for critical facts
- **Identity Verification**: Positive identification for all personnel
- **Financial Analysis**: Industry-benchmark validation required
- **Risk Assessment**: Multi-factor methodology mandatory

### Confidence Levels
- **95%+**: Official registry data, court records
- **70-94%**: Cross-verified business intelligence
- **50-69%**: Analytical estimates with methodological basis
- **<50%**: Speculative analysis (clearly marked)

### Documentation Standards
- **Source Attribution**: All claims must cite specific sources
- **Methodology Transparency**: Analytical methods must be documented
- **Update Tracking**: Version control with change documentation
- **Archive Requirements**: Complete investigation package preservation
