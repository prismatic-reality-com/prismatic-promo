## Overview

The reputation-risk-specialist operates as an L3 [Strategic Command](/glossary/strategic-command/) authority within the Prismatic Platform's reputation intelligence domain, providing continuous monitoring and risk assessment of entity reputation across digital and traditional media environments. This agent aggregates sentiment signals from news media, social platforms, regulatory filings, litigation records, and public discourse to construct comprehensive reputation profiles with quantified [risk score](/glossary/risk-score/)s. Its outputs enable proactive identification of reputational threats before they escalate into material business impact.

Built on the [AIAD](/glossary/aiad/) standard, this agent applies [NABLA Infinity](/glossary/nabla-infinity/) [contradiction preservation](/glossary/contradiction-preservation/) rigorously -- when conflicting reputation signals emerge, both the positive and negative indicators are maintained and surfaced rather than averaged into a misleading composite. The [NO DOUBTS](/glossary/no-doubts/) principle ensures that reputation assessments are grounded in verifiable evidence rather than subjective sentiment, and all risk scores carry explicit confidence intervals derived from source quality and signal diversity [metrics](/glossary/metrics/).

## Operational Domain

The reputation intelligence domain encompasses media monitoring, sentiment analysis, litigation tracking, regulatory action surveillance, and social media discourse analysis. The agent maintains continuous reputation profiles that evolve in real-time as new signals arrive. Risk assessment models account for source credibility, signal velocity, narrative coherence, and historical reputation trajectory to distinguish between transient noise and material reputation events.

## Key Capabilities

- **Multi-source reputation scoring** -- Aggregates signals from news outlets, social media, regulatory databases, court records, and industry publications to produce composite reputation risk scores with source-level attribution
- **Threat early warning** -- Detects emerging reputation threats through velocity analysis of negative signal clusters, identifying potential crises before mainstream amplification occurs
- **Sentiment trajectory analysis** -- Tracks reputation sentiment over time, identifying inflection points, trend reversals, and correlation with external events that drive reputation change
- **Peer benchmarking** -- Compares entity reputation metrics against industry peers, providing relative positioning and competitive reputation intelligence
- **[Autonomous operation](/capabilities/autonomous-self-healing/)** with self-directed monitoring cycles and adaptive alert thresholds
- **[Telemetry integration](/capabilities/telemetry-integration/)** for monitoring pipeline health and alert delivery metrics

## Enforcement

Reputation assessments comply with the [NO MERCY](/glossary/no-mercy/) doctrine: no incomplete profiles are published, every risk score includes full provenance, and contradiction preservation ensures that conflicting signals are surfaced transparently. All outputs must pass [Trinity Gate](/glossary/trinity-gate/) structural consistency validation.
