Executive Summary

Prismatic Platform's Executive Security Dashboard transforms cybersecurity from technical complexity into strategic business intelligence. Real-time risk metrics, financial impact analysis, and board-ready reporting enable C-suite executives to make informed decisions, communicate with stakeholders, and demonstrate cybersecurity ROI.

  • Real-time Risk Visibility: Strategic cybersecurity metrics updated continuously

Executive Value: Transform cybersecurity from cost center to strategic advantage with measurable business outcomes and risk-based decision making.

1.2 Dashboard Design Principles

Executive-Focused Design:

Information Hierarchy:

  1. At-a-Glance Status: Green/Yellow/Red health indicators
  2. Strategic Metrics: Key performance indicators with trend analysis
  3. Financial Impact: Cost avoidance, ROI, and business value metrics
  4. Actionable Insights: Recommendations for executive decision making
  5. Drill-Down Capability: Detailed analysis available on demand

Visual Design Standards:

  • Executive Color Palette: Professional blue/green/red with neutral backgrounds
  • Minimal Text: Graphics and visualizations prioritized over text explanations
  • Mobile Responsive: Accessible on executive mobile devices and tablets
  • Print Ready: Board meeting materials automatically generated
  • Brand Consistency: Corporate branding and visual identity maintained
