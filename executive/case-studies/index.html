Case Study 1: Global Manufacturing Enterprise

Company Profile

  • Industry: Advanced Manufacturing & Industrial IoT
  • Size: 45,000 employees across 23 countries
  • Revenue: $12B annually
  • Challenge: Complex attack surface with OT/IT convergence risks

Business Challenge

The Situation: This Fortune 500 manufacturer faced escalating cybersecurity challenges as they digitized manufacturing operations across global facilities. Their traditional security approach couldn't handle the complexity of converged OT/IT environments, cloud infrastructure, and supply chain connectivity.

Specific Pain Points:

  • 50+ manufacturing facilities with mixed legacy and modern systems
  • 200+ third-party integrations with limited visibility
  • Manual security processes taking 30+ FTE across security teams
  • Average 45-day breach detection time
  • $8M annual cybersecurity spend with poor ROI visibility
  • Regulatory compliance challenges across multiple jurisdictions

The Catalyst: A near-miss ransomware attack on a critical production line exposed gaps in their security monitoring and incident response capabilities. The potential $50M impact from production shutdown created urgency for autonomous security solutions.

Solution Implementation

Deployment Approach:

  • Phase 1 (Months 1-3): Core platform deployment across critical manufacturing sites
  • Phase 2 (Months 4-6): Extension to all facilities and supply chain monitoring
  • Phase 3 (Months 7-12): Advanced autonomous response and compliance automation

Key Configuration:

  • 434 autonomous agents monitoring 50+ facilities
  • Integration with 15 existing security tools
  • Custom adapters for industrial control systems
  • Real-time supply chain risk monitoring
  • Automated compliance reporting for 8 regulatory frameworks

Quantified Business Outcomes

Operational Efficiency Gains:

Security Team Optimization:
- Before: 30 FTE security analysts and engineers
- After: 8 FTE focused on strategic initiatives
- Personnel Cost Savings: $2.8M annually
- Productivity Improvement: 375%

Threat Detection Performance:
- Mean Time to Detection: 45 days → 2.3 hours (98% improvement)
- Mean Time to Response: 72 hours → 15 minutes (99.6% improvement)
- False Positive Reduction: 85% reduction in alert volume
- Security Incident Frequency: 15/month → 2/month (87% reduction)

Risk Mitigation Value:

Breach Prevention:
- Estimated Annual Breach Risk: $15M (historical analysis)
- Risk Reduction Achieved: 88%
- Annual Risk Mitigation Value: $13.2M

Supply Chain Security:
- Third-party Security Incidents Prevented: 12 (first year)

- Supply Chain Visibility Improvement: 95%

Regulatory Compliance:
- Compliance Automation: 85% of reporting processes

Financial Performance:
